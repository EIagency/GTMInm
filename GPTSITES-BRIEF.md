# GTM in Motion: GPT Sites implementation brief

Build an enterprise-grade, responsive B2B GTM engineering agency landing page for **GTM in Motion** at gtminmotion.com. The audience is enterprise marketing leaders, CROs, VP Sales, RevOps leaders and B2B technology organisations.

## Visual direction
Deep charcoal #10191B, off-white #F5F5EF, fresh lime #D5EE83, muted sage accents. Modern, minimalist, premium strategic consultancy aesthetic. Manrope/DM Sans-style typography. Crisp white-space, strong typographic hierarchy, restrained microinteractions, cards with clear borders. Avoid cliché stock photos or fake trust logos.

## Hero
Headline: **Make growth a system. Not a scramble.**
Subhead: We design and operationalise intelligent go-to-market engines that connect your data, teams and workflows, so enterprise growth moves with precision.
Primary CTA: **Architect your GTM engine** (link to enquiry form). Secondary CTA: **See how we work**.
Include sophisticated illustrative GTM system visual: Market signals + Account intelligence -> AI GTM orchestration -> Revenue execution, labeled conceptual blueprint (not actual metrics).

## Sections
1. Your growth strategy is only as strong as the system behind it.
2. Built for the complexity of scale: GTM strategy and systems architecture; AI agents and intelligent automation; account-based growth systems; revenue operations and integration.
3. Brand statement: Less friction. More forward motion.
4. Four-stage operating model: Diagnose -> Architect -> Activate -> Optimise.
5. Who we partner with: enterprise and mid-market B2B; SaaS and technology; professional and complex services.
6. Responsible execution: human-directed, system-aware, measurement-led.
7. Common B2B technology environments: Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, Snowflake, LinkedIn, Slack. Do not imply partnerships.
8. Common questions FAQ.
9. Enterprise enquiry form with name, work email, company, priority and business challenge.

## Conversion and trust requirements
Use a sticky-feeling nav aesthetic (can be normal positioning), CTA to consultation, accessible mobile menu, easy-to-scan content, restrained animations, real integrations only if verified, no invented enterprise client logos, certifications, results or testimonials. Connect the form to a real backend/CRM before launch. Never claim it is live when it is not.

The full HTML/CSS/JS implementation is included in the accompanying site folder and is the canonical design/content reference.
