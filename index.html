08 / COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

What does a GTM engineering engagement actually include?+

Every engagement starts with your commercial objectives and existing stack. A typical scope could include process discovery, account and signal strategy, CRM and automation architecture, implementation, governance and measurement. We define deliverables together before work begins.

Do we need to replace our CRM or existing tools?+

Usually not. Our starting point is the technology you already have. We identify where connected workflows, data quality and thoughtful automation can improve execution before recommending any new software.

How do you approach AI governance and data access?+

We scope data permissions, system boundaries, approval checkpoints and human oversight during solution design. Any security, privacy and compliance requirements are agreed with your team before implementation.

Is this a consulting engagement or managed service?+

We can structure work around architecture and implementation, or discuss ongoing optimisation where appropriate. We begin with a discovery conversation to understand your internal capabilities and priorities.
